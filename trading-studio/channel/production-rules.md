# Channel production rules (every video)

These apply to every video made with the `directing-stickman-videos` skill (B1 Minimal Line Art).

## 1. Main character: always the original
- Use **my original character**: solid black silhouette head, spiky swept black hair, two round white eyes, no mouth at rest.
  Reference: `main-character-reference.png`.
- In B1 scenes the head, hair and eyes stay exactly as the reference. Only the pose and expression change (white brow/mouth strokes for strong emotion). Pose draft: `main-character-b1-poses-draft.png`.
- The rest of each frame follows B1: near-white paper with a faint grid, black pen linework, a few red / green / yellow accents, hand-drawn props, captions in the bottom band.
- Never redesign, recolor or swap the character between scenes or videos.

## 2. Every scene backs up the script
- Each shot shows the specific thing being said at that moment (the exact chart, rule, emotion, mistake or number), not a generic trading image.
- If a line has no matching picture, rewrite the shot (or flag the line), never fill it with decoration.
- Do not show facts, numbers or claims the script doesn't contain.

## 3. Every image is unique
- No image is reused, repeated or lightly edited across shots in the same video (the character stays the same; the pose, action, props and composition must differ).
- No recycling of images from earlier videos.
- Check the contact sheet before the full render: any two near-identical frames are redone.

## 4. Thumbnail & Publishing Kit for every video
After I approve the final video, produce the skill's **Thumbnail & Publishing Kit** without asking:
1. Thumbnail, 1280×720 (16:9), with the original character, one true conflict from the video, a short accurate headline, high contrast, readable on a phone.
2. Five title options.
3. Description, tags, and chapters only when they match the final cut.
4. One pinned comment.
5. Pre-publish checklist (readability, fidelity, timestamps, music/SFX/image rights).

Saved to `publishing/` in each video's project folder. Nothing is ever uploaded or published automatically.
