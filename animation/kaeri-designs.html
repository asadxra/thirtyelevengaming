MEET KAERI

Ten styles for the same new trader. They all move, and the buttons switch every character's expression at once, so you can see how each style handles emotion. Pick one, or mix two.

1Flat Round

Closest to the reference video. Fastest to animate and stays clean at phone size.

2Chibi Anime

Big head, sparkly eyes, spiky hair with a teal streak. Very expressive and popular with gaming audiences.

3Retro Rubber Hose

A 1930s cartoon feel: noodle arms, pie-cut eyes, gloves and a gold cap. Bouncy and has a vintage charm.

4Blocky Geometric

Square shapes and pixel-style eyes. Fits a gaming brand and is easy to keep consistent across episodes.

5Soft Blob

A squishy, cute mascot with no limbs to animate. Easy to make into stickers and merch, with a calmer, cozy vibe.

68-Bit Pixel

A retro game sprite. Instantly says "gaming" and suits level-up and game-over moments in the story.

7Paper Cutout

Shapes cut from colored paper on kraft card, with real drop shadows. Handmade and warm, like a craft stop-motion.

8Notebook Doodle

Wobbly marker lines on lined paper, like it was drawn in class. Feels personal and funny, and cheap to make.

9Neon Line

Glowing outlines on a synthwave grid, with a visor and a chart that lights up green or red. Night-trading energy.

10Bull Mascot

Kaeri as a young bull in a hoodie, a nod to the "bull market". A strong brand mascot; a bear rival could show up later.
