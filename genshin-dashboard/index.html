Shorts tips built into every script
  • Open with the payoff in the first 2 seconds, with the hook also as big on-screen text.
  • 25 to 40 seconds, one topic, something new on screen every 2 to 3 seconds, no filler words.
  • Show a few cards long enough to read, with one takeaway per section, and captions in the middle third.
  • End with one specific question.

Title and thumbnail tips
  • Title: the character name plus a promise or curiosity. The generator now does this for you, for example "Furina BEST Team".
  • Thumbnail: the character's face or a big pose, three to four words at most, with high contrast.
  • Comment question: one specific question gets far more comments than "let me know below". The generator now ends each caption with one.
