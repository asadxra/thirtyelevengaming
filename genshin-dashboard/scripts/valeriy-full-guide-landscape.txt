[0s-62s CHAPTER 1: CHARACTER INTRODUCTION & PULL VALUE]
[Start Introduction]
One 4-star Electro shield can lift the damage of a whole Stellar-Conduct team, and most players never build him for it. Stay to the end and you will know which weapon, artifacts and teams make Valeriy worth your time, and whether he deserves a spot on your account.

Valeriy is a 4-star Electro Sword user who plays as a support. His Elemental Skill puts a long Electro shield on the active character, and the shield strength scales with his Attack. His Burst calls down Thunderburst Shells that hit enemies in an area. Between those two, he protects your carry and feeds the team Attack-based buffs.

His home is Stellar-Conduct teams. When a party member triggers Superconduct, it turns into Stellar-Conduct inside a Polestar Field, and Valeriy boosts that Electro damage. That is why he pairs so well with Mitya, Odette and Cyno.

What he does well is simple. He gives you a strong shield and buffs that reach the whole team, and his 4-star rarity makes him easy to obtain and easy to build. Where he falls short is just as clear, because he needs Stellar-Conduct or Electro teams to shine and does little on his own. Pull or build him if you run Mitya, Cyno or Odette, or if you want a shield support for Clorinde. Valeriy debuted in version 7.2, so some rankings can still shift after more testing.
[End Introduction]

[62s-74s CHAPTER 1: CONSTELLATIONS]
[Start Constellations]
Constellations decide how far you push him, and each level does something different. Before we go through them one by one, here is what you need to know.

Constellations are the upgrades you unlock by pulling duplicates of Valeriy, one level per duplicate, up to a maximum of six. You do not need any of them to use him. Everything in this guide works at Constellation 0, and he is a perfectly good shield and buff support without a single one. But his constellations are worth knowing, because they change how much he gives your team.

Generally speaking, they fall into three groups. Constellation 1 and Constellation 4 improve his Charged Attack and his Energy, so they help his own rotation. Constellation 3 and Constellation 5 raise his Normal Attack and Elemental Skill levels, so they make the talents you already level stronger. And Constellation 2 and Constellation 6 are the two that buff the team, which means those are the ones that matter most for how good he is next to Mitya, Cyno or Clorinde.

Constellation 2 is the biggest jump. It gives nearby party members 100 Elemental Mastery and turns extra Impact Momentum into shield strength, so it is the first one worth aiming for. Constellation 6 is the strongest overall, adding 100 percent damage to his special Charged Attack and his Thunderburst Shells, plus extra Electro Crit Rate and Crit Damage on enemies he hits. Since he is a 4-star, he is easier to get duplicates for than a 5-star, so reaching Constellation 2 is realistic for most players.

Keep in mind that these numbers come from version 7.2 data, so check them in game. Here is what each constellation gives you, in order.
[End Constellations]

[74s-93s CHAPTER 1: CONSTELLATION 1]
[Start Constellation 1]
The first constellation is all about Energy, and that matters because Valeriy's Burst costs 60 Energy, which is a lot for a support. Without help, he needs a good amount of Energy Recharge or a teammate who feeds him particles before he can use it every rotation. Constellation 1 fixes much of that problem from inside his own kit. It also makes his special Charged Attack easier to reach, so his shield and buffs come online sooner at the start of a fight. That makes it a quiet but useful upgrade, and the first step toward the bigger team buffs that come later.

Constellation 1 refunds Energy. After his special Charged Attack, he regains 6 Elemental Energy. If he spends more than 40 Impact Momentum, he gains 1.5 more Energy for every 10 extra points, up to 15 Energy per use, once every 15 seconds. The first Skill of each fight also grants 150 percent Impact Momentum, so his Charged Attack is ready faster.
[End Constellation 1]

[93s-110s CHAPTER 1: CONSTELLATION 2]
[Start Constellation 2]
The biggest jump comes next.

Constellation 2 buffs the team. For 18 seconds after his Skill, nearby party members gain 100 Elemental Mastery. And while he is shielded at maximum Impact Momentum, the extra momentum turns into shield strength, 8 percent of his Attack per point, up to 20 points. This is the biggest jump in his kit.
[End Constellation 2]

Constellation 6 adds 100 percent damage to his special Charged Attack and his Thunderburst Shells. Enemies he hits also take 10 percent more Electro Crit Rate for 7 seconds, and Stellar-Conduct reaction damage from those enemies gains 40 percent Crit Damage.

He works fine at zero constellations, and Constellation 2 gives the biggest jump. These numbers come from version 7.2 data, so check them in game.

[167s-229s CHAPTER 2: TALENT MECHANICS]
[Start Talent Mechanics]
Level the wrong talent and you waste books. His kit comes first, then the order that pays off.

His Normal Attack is up to three sword strikes. His Charged Attack leaps and slams the ground for Physical area damage. But when he holds at least 40 Impact Momentum, the Charged Attack turns into a special Charged Attack that spends all of it for a bigger hit. That is his damage engine.

His Elemental Skill deals Electro area damage, gives him 40 Impact Momentum, and puts up the Supercharged Shield. The shield strength scales with his Attack, and it absorbs Electro damage at 250 percent efficiency. So a high Attack stat makes the shield sturdier.

His Elemental Burst deals Electro area damage, then fires a Thunderburst Shell that splits into a barrage. While the shells fall, each time a nearby party member hits an enemy with Electro, he gains 5 Impact Momentum, up to 80 per Burst. That means Electro teammates fill his Charged Attack for him.

Two passives matter. Each Impact Momentum he spends on the special Charged Attack raises the damage bonus of his Offensive Directive, by 0.9 percent of his Attack inside Stellar-Conduct and 0.3 percent otherwise. And he enters the Stellar-Conduct state while he stands in a Polestar Field.
[End Talent Mechanics]

Weapons next. I will go through every weapon in his build guide: what it gives him, why it works, where it falls short, and who should use it. Valeriy is a 4-star with no signature weapon, so there is no single must-have pick.

One rule before we start. He scales with Attack, because his shield and buffs scale with Attack, and he also needs enough Energy Recharge to cast his 60 Energy Burst. So every weapon has to pass one check: it gives him Attack or Energy Recharge, and it does not fight his support role.
